You know Japanese?

Eh, mostly. I started in March 2020 with lockdown and learned the basics. I took a class that summer. I stopped when senior year started and had to do college application stuff. But I would still see the Japanese language everyday because of Twitter and watching youtubers like Noriyaro and AbroadInJapan. I eventually enrolled in a Japanese 101 class in college for a degree requirement, but I made the decision to get a minor in Japanese because I just felt like it. It wasn't going to get in my way or anything, so why not? The minor in Japanese is half language and half Japanese history so I'm pretty excited

Hey, Om from 2026 here. Now that I'm only 2 weeks away from graduating (May 14th 2026), I can talk about my experience. I've gotten a lot better at Japanese now that I'm in my 6th year (if we count me starting in March 2020) of learning it. Signing up for the Japanese minor definitely helped a bunch. It's the only reason I even continued to even learn this language. Along with the language class I took the kanji which also helped out quite a bit since I'm not that good at writing kanji. It helped me remember some kanji, but most of all it helped me get better at writing it. With this, I can whole-heartedly say that minoring in Japanese was probably one of the best I've made ever.
