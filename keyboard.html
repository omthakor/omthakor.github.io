Wooting 80 HE

Vortex Angle TKL (Gateron Oil Kings)

i have the black case. great sounding keyboard with the oil kings but there is a software issue. when putting my pc to sleep with the keyboard plugged in and waking it up, it'll spam one key or it just doesn't work. unplugging and replugging fixes it but it's just annoying.

Akko 5087S (Gateron Zero Degree Silent switch, ePBT keycaps)

Deck Hassium Turtle Ship Edition (Cherry MX Red)

personal chase keyboard of mine since it was only sold in South Korea, personal favorite of mine. bought it from a guy on twitter who lives in Italy. i'd love to find the TKL version of this keyboard but it being a south korea exclusive means i have to search on sites like global bunjang and hope the seller doesn't cancel on me like when i tried to get the TKL version back in summer 2025 haha



Wooting 60 HE V2 (Split Spacebar) + Tofu60 Redux V2 Case Swap

great improvement to the original 60 HE. i went with the split spacebar option and i don't regret it at all. between the 2 spacebars, there's another fn key that i can use with my left hand and is closer to the left side of the keyboard. the tofu60 redux v2 case is great as well. it came with PET film and silicone in the case already and since the 60 he v2 is catch mount, it was really easy to switch out. it was as easy as picking up the pcb and putting it in the case. i also got new pbt cherry profile keycaps from wooting that aren't shine through and have japanese legends on them. i also swapped the original lekker tikkens with the tikken light switches that came out 2 months ago. it's a daily driver along with the pulsar pcmk3 60 he, where i use the pulsar for my linux machine and the wooting for my windows laptop for games like valorant or COD



Rainy75 (Stock)

probably the best stock sounding keyboard i've ever used. purchased it on prime day for about 90 bucks and dare i say it might be the best bang for your buck keyboard. there are other clones out there like the womier sk75 which i haven't used but i'm guessing it sounds pretty much the same for cheaper.



Nuphy Air60 HE

i've always wanted a low profile keyboard, especially for vertical rhythm games like osu mania, etterna, djmax etc. i found out i play a lot better on a laptop keyboard but i didn't want to have to use my laptop for just rhythm games. i was already pretty aware of this keyboard, since their wireless keyboards had exploding batteries a few years back, but this doesn't have a battery and will not explode. i got the gateron low profile jade pro switches and they sound great and feel great. the only nitpick i have is that the usb c port is on the left side of the keyboard. it just looks funny and it would've looked better if it were anywhere on the top



HHKB Professional 2

this keyboard was another chase of mine and it was a steal. i got it for 120 bucks off of mercari and it retails for about 220 bucks. worked perfectly find and i only use it for if i'm doing anything typing heavy. the topre switches are no joke, they feel great and it's nice to finally be able to experience them
