Wooting 60 HE

my first rapid trigger keyboard, pretty solid overall, lekker v1 switches don't sound that good and stabilizer lubing from the factory wears off pretty quick



Wooting 80 HE

second rapid trigger keyboard, lekker v2 switches were a good improvement, but stabilizer lubing problem still exists



Vortex Angle TKL (Gateron Oil Kings)

i have the black case. great sounding keyboard with the oil kings but there is a software issue. when putting my pc to sleep with the keyboard plugged in and waking it up, it'll spam one key or it just doesn't work. unplugging and replugging fixes it but it's just annoying.



Pulsar PCMK2 HE TKL

nice out of box experience



Pulsar PCMK3 60 HE

once again, nice out of box experience, i will say that it sounds different compared to the PCMK2 TKL in a good way. the switches are the same as the PCMK2 but the small form factor makes it sound different i'm guessing.



Akko 5087S (Gateron Zero Degree Silent switch, ePBT keycaps)

wrong keycaps in picture, but it's my favorite silent keyboard. i am thinking of getting switches that are even more silent though



Monsgeek M7 (Kailh Speed Silver)

the brick. great keyboard, space bar stablilzer lube did wear off quickly though



Deck Hassium Turtle Ship Edition (Cherry MX Red)

personal chase keyboard of mine since it was only sold in South Korea, personal favorite of mine. bought it from a guy on twitter who lives in Italy. i'd love to find the TKL version of this keyboard but it being a south korea exclusive means i have to search on sites like global bunjang and hope the seller doesn't cancel on me like when i tried to get the TKL version back in summer 2025 haha



Rainy75 (Stock)

probably the best stock sounding keyboard i've ever used. purchased it on prime day for about 90 bucks and dare i say it might be the best bang for your buck keyboard. there are other clones out there like the womier sk75 which i haven't used but i'm guessing it sounds pretty much the same for cheaper.
